Documentary Requirements Checklist

Please prepare digital copies of the required civil and municipal clearances to ensure smooth staff review and verification.

Death Certificate

Certified True Copy from the Local Civil Registry (LCR) or Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) indicating decedent details and cause of death.

Mandatory Civil Document

Burial or Transfer Permit

Official clearance permit issued by the City Health Office (CHO) or municipal sanitary health officer authorizing interment or transport.

Municipal Health Clearance

Valid Government-Issued ID

Clear, unexpired government identification of the next-of-kin, authorized family representative, or legal claimant filing the request.

Informant Identification
Service-Specific Documentation Notice

Required documents may vary depending on the selected service (e.g. cremation permits or exhumation clearances). Users will be guided through the applicable requirements during the online booking process.
