What the system does

Everything a cemetery needs to run day to day

One system built specifically for cemetery administration — from the first request to the completed record.

Burial Scheduling

Coordinate burial dates and services with a clear, conflict-free schedule.

Lot & Capacity Management

Track lot availability across the grounds, with forecasting to plan ahead as sections fill.

Decedent Records

Keep accurate, organized records tied to each lot and service.

Payments & Reservations

Handle reservations and payments in one place, with a clear record of every transaction.
