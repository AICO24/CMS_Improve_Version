The Process

From Service Selection to Verification & Payment

A structured, transparent four-step workflow designed to ensure complete accuracy, civil registry compliance, and validated records.

1

Create an Account

Register or sign in as a User, then choose between ground burial plot reservation or cremation sanctuary services.

2

Submit Request & Requirements

Provide decedent and schedule information, and upload the necessary documentary requirements for cemetery review.

3

Staff Verification

Cemetery administration reviews and verifies the submitted documents and scheduling availability before the booking advances.

4

Payment & Confirmation

After staff clearance, proceed through the secure online payment flow to receive your official digital receipt voucher.
